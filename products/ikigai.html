Title: Ikigai

Author: Héctor García & Francesc Miralles

Genre: Self-help, lifestyle

Published: 2016

"Ikigai"

₱100.00

Ikigai introduces the Japanese concept of finding purpose through the things you love, the skills you develop, the people around you, and the activities that give your life meaning. Drawing from Japanese culture, particularly the lifestyle of the people of Okinawa, the book discusses habits, relationships, work, and ways of living that can contribute to a longer and more fulfilling life. It is a thoughtful read for anyone interested in discovering their purpose and creating a more meaningful everyday life.
